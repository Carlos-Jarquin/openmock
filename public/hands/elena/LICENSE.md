Modifications: the FBX deform skeleton was flattened and renamed to the WebXR generic-hand joint names, scaled so the wrist-to-middle-fingertip length is 178 mm, mirrored to make a left hand, and exported as meshopt-compressed glTF. The specular map was lifted so the skin keeps a satin highlight. Control bones that carried no skin weights were removed. Details are in `README.md` in this directory.
